THE BRAND

BUILT FOR PEOPLE WHO MOVE.

Red Bull is an energy drink brand founded in 1987. It has developed a strong identity around energy, action, sport, adventure, music and entertainment.

The brand is recognised for its distinctive cans, bold visual identity and the famous slogan “Gives You Wings.”

This website is an independent Year 12 school advertising project showing how product presentation, interactive design and branding can work together to create a memorable digital experience.
